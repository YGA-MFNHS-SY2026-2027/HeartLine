What HeartLine actually is

HeartLine is run by Youth Guidance Advocates (YGA), a student-led initiative focused on supporting anyone who feels hesitant or afraid to speak up. Our mission is to help students navigate personal and academic challenges, work toward their goals, and take care of their overall well-being.

YGA is not the Discipline Office. We don't handle code-of-conduct violations or behavior management — our focus is entirely on personal development, emotional support, and guidance, so every student can thrive in a safe environment.

Every message goes to a real person: our Guidance Counselor Designate or a trained YGA volunteer. No bots, no scripts read off a screen — just someone from your own school who can help you figure out the next step, whether that's a listening ear, a referral, or just checking in again tomorrow.

Small steps while you wait to talk

These won't replace a real conversation, but they can help in the meantime.

Name it out loud

Saying or writing what you're feeling, even in one sentence, takes some of its weight away.

Find one person nearby

A friend, teacher, or family member you trust — you don't have to explain everything at once.

Step outside for a bit

Fresh air and a short walk can loosen a thought spiral, even briefly.
